$(B, N)$-Pairs
:::definition
We say that two subgroups $B$ and $N$ of a group $G$ form a $(B,N)$-pair for $G$ if
- $B$ and $N$ generate $G$ and $T:=B\cap N$ is normal in $N$.
- The group $W:=N/T$ is generated by a set $S$ of involutions such that:
  - For $s\in S, w\in W$ we have $BsB.BwB\subset BwB\cup BswB$.
  - For $s\in S$, we have $sBs\nsubseteq B$.
:::

For a connected reductive group $G$, and $T\subset B$ is a pair of a maximal torus and a Borel subgroup, then $(B, N_G(T))$ is a $(B, N)$-pair for $G$. We omit the proof.

:::theorem
If $G$ has a $(B,N)$-pair, then
$$
G = \bigsqcup_{w\in W} BwB.
$$
:::
:::proof
$G$ is generated by $B, N$, then $G=\bigcup_{i}(BNB)^i$. 
- $BNB=BWB$.

It suffices to show
$$
BWBWB=BWB \impliedby BwBWB\subset BWB.\\
\impliedby Bs_1B.Bs_2B.....Bs_nB.BWB\subset BWB\\
\impliedby BsB.BWB\subset BWB
$$
But we have
$$
BsB.BwB\subset BwB\cup BswB\subset BWB.
$$
The next lemma finishes the proof.
:::
:::lemma
It is left to show that if $w\neq w'$, $BwB\neq Bw'B$.
:::
:::proof
Induction on the length of $\inf(\ell(w), \ell(w'))$. 

Assume $\ell(w)\leq \ell(w')$. 

If $\ell(w)=0$, $Bw'B\neq B$ (since $w'\notin B$).

If $\ell(w)>0$, find $s\in S$, such that $\ell(sw)=\ell(w)-1$. Then $$\ell(sw)<\ell(w)\leq\ell(w')\qquad \text{ and}\quad sw\neq sw'.$$ By induction, $$BswB\neq Bw'B\quad \text{ and} \quad BswB\neq Bsw'B.$$ Then $BswB\cap BsB.Bw'B=\emptyset$, since $BsB.Bw'B\subset Bw'B\cup Bsw'B$. 

$Bsw'B\subset BsB.Bw'B$, then $BswB\neq Bsw'B=\emptyset$.
:::
