

:::theorem Wedderburn Theorem.

A Finite Division Ring Must Be Commutative.

:::

Let $D$ be a finite division ring, and F be its center. Then 

- $(D:F)$ is a square.

- $x\in D$, then $x\in L\subset D$ with a field $L$, and $(L:F)^2=(D:F)$.

- All such $L$ above are conjugate (Skolem-Noether Theorem).

Let $L$ be one such field, and $H=L^{\times}$, $G=D^{\times}$. Obviously, we have  $G=\bigcup_{g\in G}\mathrm{ad}(g)(H)$. The next theorem says $G=H$, and $D=L$.


:::theorem 

For a group, may be infinite, and $H < G$ with $(G : H) < \infty$. If $H$ intesects with every conjugacy class of $G$, then $H = G$.

:::
